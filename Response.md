Why the lack of security details is a serious issue
1. Sensitive personal data requires a secure process
A driver’s license contains:
- Full legal name
- Address
- Date of birth
- DL number
- Photo
Any company collecting this must provide:
- A secure upload portal
- A privacy policy
- A data‑handling explanation
- A retention policy
- A statement that it will not be shared
If they can’t provide these, they should not be collecting your ID.
2. Recruiters almost never need your ID before an offer
Legitimate ID requests happen only:
- After you sign an offer
- During official background checks
- Through a known vendor (HireRight, Sterling, etc.)
- Inside a secure system
A recruiter asking for it early, by email, or without security details is not normal.
3. You are responsible for protecting your identity
If your DL is leaked or misused, you—not the recruiter—deal with:
- Identity theft
- Fraudulent accounts
- Address exposure
- Credit issues
You’re right to reject the request.

You can respond with something like:
For security reasons, I don’t provide copies of my driver’s license outside of a formal background‑check process. If ID verification is required later in the hiring process, I’m happy to provide it through the client’s official onboarding system.
