To disable the automatic electronic door lock on most Panasonic microwave models, 
	press **Turbo Defrost** (or **Auto Defrost**), 
	followed immediately by **Timer/Clock** (or **Timer**), and 
	then **Popcorn** in sequence. 

A long beep and the display showing **OFF** indicate the lock is deactivated; repeating this sequence reactivates it.
