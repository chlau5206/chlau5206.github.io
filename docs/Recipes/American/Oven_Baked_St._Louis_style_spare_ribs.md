## oven-baked St. Louis style spare ribs:  

Because St. Louis ribs are cut from the belly side of the pig, they are meatier, flatter, and contain more fat and collagen than baby back ribs. This extra fat makes them rich and juicy, but requires slightly more time low-and-slow to tenderize.

**Oven Temperature & Cooking Times:**

- **Primary Method (Low & Slow at 275°F / 135°C)**: Bake tightly wrapped in aluminum foil for **3 to 3.5 hours** (about 30 to 60 minutes longer than baby back ribs).
- **Moderate Bake (300°F / 150°C)**: Bake wrapped in foil for **2.5 to 3 hours**.
- **Sauce Finish (350°F / 175°C or Broiler)**: Open the foil, brush liberally with BBQ sauce, and return to the oven for **15 to 30 minutes** to caramelize the sauce into a sticky glaze.

**Key Preparation & Techniques:**

1. **Remove the Membrane**: Peel off the tough silver skin membrane on the back/bone side of the rack so seasonings absorb and the meat stays tender.
2. **Double Foil Wrap**: Wrap the rack tightly in heavy-duty aluminum foil (meat-side up or down on a rimmed sheet pan) to trap steam and slowly break down the connective tissue.
3. **Doneness Test**: The ribs are done when the meat has pulled back about ½ inch from the ends of the bones and yields easily when pierced with a fork (internal temperature around 190°F–202°F).
