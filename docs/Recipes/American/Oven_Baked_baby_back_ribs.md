## Oven-baked baby back ribs time and temperature:  

The ideal method for baking baby back ribs in the oven is cooking low and slow at **275°F (135°C) for 2.5 to 3 hours**, wrapped tightly in aluminum foil to lock in moisture and yield fall-off-the-bone tenderness.

**Key cooking methods and times:**

1. **Low and Slow (275°F for 2.5–3 hours)**: The standard technique recommended by **Inspired Taste**, **Cooking For My Soul**, and **Kinder's** — tightly foil-wrapping the ribs at 275°F allows the connective tissue to break down slowly without drying out the meat.
2. **Moderate Oven (300°F–325°F for 2 to 2.5 hours)**: Suggested by **Valerie's Kitchen** and **Delicious by Design** for a slightly faster bake while maintaining tenderness.
3. **High Heat Fast Track (400°F for ~1 hour)**: Covered by **Fresh Fork Market**, baking wrapped ribs for 50 minutes at 400°F, then saucing uncovered for 10–15 minutes.
4. **Broiler/Sauce Finish**: Across all methods, open or unwrap the foil for the last 5–10 minutes (or place under the broiler on high) after brushing with BBQ sauce to caramelize the glaze.
