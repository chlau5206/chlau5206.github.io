Roasted Lamb Leg
================
Recipe inclued 3 Rubs options, and 2 roast methods. 
Rubs: 
	1. traditional: rosemary and thyme.
	2. Mint: traditional + mint
	3. Italian: Italian seasoning
Roasts:
	1. High-Heat Roasting
	2. Low and slow cooking

Ingredients
2.5 lb leg of lamb
2 cloves of garlic, sliced
Rub:
Option #1:
	1 teaspoon dried rosemary (or 1 tablespoon fresh, chopped)
	1 teaspoon dried thyme (or 1 tablespoon fresh, chopped)
Option #2:
	add: 1 tablespoon dried mint (optional)
Option #3: 
	2 tablespoons dried Italian seasoning
2 tablespoons brown sugar
1/2 lemon, zested and juiced
2 tablespoons olive oil
Salt and pepper, to taste
Gravy:
1/2 cup red wine (optional)
1/2 cup beef or chicken broth

Instructions
Preheat Oven: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
(Optional:) Low-and-Slow Medium-Rare Roasted Lamb Leg : Preheat your oven to 275°F (135°C).

1. Prepare the Lamb: Make small incisions all over the lamb leg and insert the garlic slices into the cuts.

2. Season: In a small bowl, mix the rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper. 
Rub this mixture all over the lamb.

3. Roast: Place the lamb in a roasting pan and roast in the preheated oven.

4. For medium-rare, cook for about 20 minutes per pound (internal temperature of 135°F or 57°C).
	(Optional:)Roast: Place the lamb in a roasting pan and cover it tightly with aluminum foil.
				Cook: Roast the lamb at 275°F for about 3-4 hours, or until the internal temperature reaches 
				around 135°F (57°C) for medium-rare.

5. For medium, cook for about 25 minutes per pound (internal temperature of 145°F or 63°C).

6. Rest: Once done, remove the lamb from the oven and let it rest for about 15 minutes before carving.

Optional 
1. Gravy: While the lamb rests, you can make a gravy. Pour the red wine (if using) and broth into 
the roasting pan. Place the pan over medium heat and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom. Let it 
simmer until reduced by half.
2. mint is a wonderful addition to lamb recipes! It brings a fresh, vibrant flavor that pairs beautifully 
with the rich taste of the lamb. Here's how you can incorporate mint into your roasted lamb leg:

Mint and Herb Rub
Use the same recipe as before but add 1 tablespoon of chopped fresh mint leaves to the herb mixture 
(or 1 teaspoon of dried mint).

Mix the mint with the rosemary, thyme, lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Rub this 
mixture all over the lamb before roasting.

Mint Sauce
You can also serve the lamb with a classic mint sauce. Here's a simple recipe:

Ingredients
1/2 cup fresh mint leaves, finely chopped
2 tablespoons sugar
1/4 cup hot water
1/4 cup white vinegar
Salt, to taste

Instructions
1. Combine Mint and Sugar: In a bowl, mix the chopped mint leaves and sugar.
2. Add Hot Water: Pour the hot water over the mint and sugar, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
3. Add Vinegar: Stir in the white vinegar and add a pinch of salt to taste.
4. Serve: Let the sauce sit for about 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld, then serve alongside the roasted lamb.

Marinations:
Quick Marination
Time: Let the seasoned lamb sit at room temperature for about 30 minutes to an hour before roasting. This will help the flavors start to meld with the meat.

Longer Marination
Time: For even more flavor, you can marinate the lamb in the refrigerator for several hours or overnight. Just cover it with plastic wrap or place it in a sealable plastic bag. When you're ready to cook, take it out of the fridge and let it come to room temperature for about 30 minutes before roasting.
