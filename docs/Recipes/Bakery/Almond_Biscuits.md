### Almond Biscuits (Ricciarelli)

2 	 cup 	Almond Flour
2 	 tsp 	Baking Power
0.5  tsp 	Salt ( too much salt, should reduce)
2 			Eggs
0.33 cup 	Butter

---------------------------------------------------------
https://healthyrecipesblogs.com/almond-flour-biscuits/
Ingredients:
1     		large egg
.25  cup	sour cream
.25  tsp	Sea salt
1 	 cup    Almond flour (4 oz)
2  	tsp 	baking powder

Instructions:
1. 	Preheat oven to 350 degrees F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. 	In a medium bowl, whisk together the egg, sour cream and salt. Using a rubber spatula, mix in the almond flour, then the baking powder.
3. 	Using a 2-tablespoon scoop, place 6 mounds of the dough on the prepared baking sheet. Do not flatten the mounds.
4. 	Bake until golden, 15-18 minutes. Serve warm.
