SOFT BUTTERMILK BISCUITS 
https://youtu.be/37JzQUooogw

INGREDIENTS
2 cups (300 g) all purpose flour
1 Tbsp baking powder (14 g)
1 1/4 tsp salt (6 g)
1 tsp sugar (5 g)
8 Tbsp (113 g) cold unsalted butter
8 fl oz (236 ml) cold buttermilk (or whole milk with 1 Tbsp white vinegar or fresh lemon juice) 
2 Tbsp extra buttermilk or melted better to brush biscuit tops
BAKE @ 425 º F / 218 º C for 15 to 17 minutes
