Smoothie King Angel Food Smoothie Copycat
(https://thefoodxp.com/smoothie-king-angel-food-smoothie-copycat-recipe/)

INGREDIENTS
½ cup Banana
½ cup Strawberries
2 tablespoons Milk Powder
2 tablespoons Turbinado Sugar
1 teaspoon Vanilla Extract
½ cup Water
½ cup Ice Cubes

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Take a blender, add all the elements and blend until it is smooth.
2. For better results, add water first and the frozen elements at last.
3. Blend the smoothie for just 2-4 minutes.
4. Pour the smoothie into a glass and add ice on the top.
5. Garnish the smoothie with some chopped strawberries and serve to enjoy!
