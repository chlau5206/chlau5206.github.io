Sonic Crave Sauce Copycat
(https://thefoodxp.com/sonic-crave-sauce-copycat-recipe/)
INGREDIENTS
 ½ cup Miracle Whip
1 tablespoon Ketchup
1 tablespoon Relish
1 tablespoon Garlic Powder
1 tablespoon Onion Powder
1 teaspoon Turmeric
1 teaspoon Lemon
1 teaspoon Cayenne Pepper
1 teaspoon Salt

INSTRUCTIONS
1. In a bowl, add miracle whip, ketchup, relish, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and cayenne pepper.
2. Whisk the sauce until it is smooth and flavorful.
3. The knockoff Sonic crave sauce will be ready in a jiffy.
4. Serve to enjoy with your favorite snacks.
