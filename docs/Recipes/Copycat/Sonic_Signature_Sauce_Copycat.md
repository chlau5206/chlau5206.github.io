Sonic Signature Sauce Copycat
(https://thefoodxp.com/sonic-signature-sauce-copycat-recipe/)
INGREDIENTS
1 tablespoon Mayonnaise
1 tablespoon Honey
1 tablespoon Yellow Mustard
1 tablespoon Dijon Mustard
1 tablespoon Lemon Juice
1 tablespoon Barbecue Sauce

INSTRUCTIONS
1. In a medium bowl, add mayonnaise, yellow mustard, Dijon mustard, a teaspoon of honey, and barbecue sauce.
2. Mix all of them evenly until it forms a smooth texture.
3. Sonic’s signature sauce will be ready to serve.
