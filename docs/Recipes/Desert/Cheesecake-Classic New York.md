Cheesecake-Classic New York
===========================
Total: 2 hrs
Prep: 30 mins
Cook: 90 mins
Yield: 12 to 16 Servings

If you love a wonderfully dense cheesecake, this New York cheesecake recipe is for you. We bake it at 500 F for a few minutes, then turn the oven down to 200 F and bake it the rest of the way. The result: creamy cheesecake perfection.

Ingredients
For the Crust:
10 graham crackers
1 tablespoon sugar
For the Filling:
2 1/2 pounds cream cheese (5 packs)
1 1/2 cups sugar
1/2 cup sour cream
6 whole eggs plus 2 egg yolks
1 tablespoon lemon juice
2 teaspoons vanilla extract
Pinch salt

Steps to Make It
Prepare the Graham Cracker Crust
01. Preheat oven to 325 F.
02. Break up the graham crackers and pulse them in a food processor until you have fine crumbs. While you do this you can be melting the butter in a small saucepan over low heat (or just melt it in the microwave).
03. Transfer the crumbs to a mixing bowl and stir in the sugar.
04. Finally, stir in the melted butter and combine until the crumbs are uniformly moistened.
05. Press the crumbs into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan until you have an even layer.
06. Bake for ten minutes, then remove the pan from the oven and let the crust cool while you do the next steps.

Prepare the Cheesecake
01. Preheat oven to 500 F.
02. Cut the cream cheese into cubes and let it sit at room temperature for about 20 minutes to soften.?
03. Transfer the softened cream cheese to the bowl of a stand mixer. Beat it on medium speed with the paddle attachment for about two minutes.?
04. Scrape the sides of the bowl with a rubber spatula, then add the sugar, salt, lemon juice, vanilla, and sour cream and beat for another two minutes.?
05. Scrape the bowl again, then add the eggs and egg yolks and beat until everything is completely blended and the mixture is smooth and fluffy, another couple of minutes. You may need to stop the mixer to scrape the bowl every now and then.?
06. Fill a roasting pan half full of water and set it on the lower rack of your oven. Seal the bottom of the springform pan with foil and set it on a baking pan.?
07. Now pour the filling into the springform pan and transfer the baking pan (with the cheesecake on it) to the upper rack in the oven.?
08. Bake at 500 F for 10 minutes, then lower the heat to 200 F and bake for another 1 1/2 hours. Don't open the oven door! When it's finished, the cheesecake should jiggle a little bit at the center but be firm at the edges.?
09. Transfer the cheesecake to a wire rack, and after cooling for 15 minutes, carefully loosen the edges of the cake with a knife.?
10. Let the cheesecake cool for a full three hours at room temperature, then cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate at least overnight, although a full 24 hours is best.
