Eggs Waffle Hong Kong Style

Directions:
wet
1. 3 Egg yolks + 3/4 cup sugar mix
2. + 6 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
3. + 1 tbsp vanilla extract
4. + 3/4 cup milk

Dry
5. 1 1/4 cups cake flour
6. + 1 1/2 tsp baking power
7. + Pinch of salt

Mix
8. Mix wet into dry ingredients
9. Add 1/3 of the egg whites
10. Add remaining egg whites
