MASCARPONE MOUSSE
=================
Base:
* 4oz Mascarpone cheese 
* 1 tbsp Honey
* 1/4 cup Whipped Cream
* pinch cream of Tartar
* Cocoa powder

Variation: 
- Rum-Only version
	-- 2 tbsp Rum-Only
	
- Rum + Marsala Blend
	-- 1 tbsp rum + 1 tbsp Marsala
	
- Non-Alcohol versioin
	-- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
	
- Spiced (Alcohol-free)
	-- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
	-- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
	-- Pinch of nutmeg
	--- Stir mascarpone with honey, vanilla, cinnamon, and nutmeg until smooth.

Optional: fresh blueberries or poached pears for topping
