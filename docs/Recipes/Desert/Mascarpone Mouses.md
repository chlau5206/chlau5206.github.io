# 🍯 Simple Mascarpone Mousse (Serves 2)

## Ingredients

- 4 oz mascarpone cheese (use leftover)
- 2 tbsp honey (or to taste)
- 2 tbsp rum or Marsala wine (optional, for boozy depth)
- ¼ cup heavy cream (whipped, optional for lightness)
- Cocoa powder or shaved chocolate (for garnish)
- Fresh fruit or cookie crumbs (optional layers)

## Instructions

1. **Base**: Stir mascarpone with honey until smooth. Add rum/Marsala if desired.
2. **Lighten**: Whip cream separately, then fold into mascarpone for a mousse‑like texture. (Skip cream for a denser, custard‑style mousse.)
3. **Serve**: Spoon into glasses. Dust with cocoa or top with fruit/chocolate. Chill 1–2 hours before serving.

👉 Result: Quick, versatile dessert that uses up mascarpone and pairs beautifully with espresso or leftover ladyfinger crumbs.

## 🔑 Strategy

- Use **remaining mascarpone** for mousse → no waste, easy second dessert.
