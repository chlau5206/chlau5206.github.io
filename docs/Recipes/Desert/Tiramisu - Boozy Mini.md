🍰 Boozy Mini Tiramisu (Serves 2–3)

Ingredients
- 3 egg yolks
- 1 tbsp honey (reduced sweetness)
- 4 oz mascarpone cheese (about ½ cup)
- ½ cup heavy cream (whipped with a pinch of cream of tartar)
- ½ cup strong espresso coffee (cooled)
- 2 tbsp rum
- 1 tbsp Marsala wine (optional, for depth)
- 10 ladyfingers (3 oz)
- Unsweetened cocoa powder (for dusting)

Instructions
1. **Cream base**: Whisk egg yolks with honey over a gentle bain‑marie until pale and slightly thickened. Cool, then fold in mascarpone until smooth.
2. **Whip cream**: Beat heavy cream with cream of tartar until soft peaks form. Fold gently into mascarpone mixture.
3. **Coffee soak**: Mix espresso, rum, and Marsala in a shallow dish. Dip ladyfingers quickly (1–2 seconds per side).
4. **Layer**: Arrange 5 soaked ladyfingers in a small dish. Spread half the cream. Repeat with remaining 5 ladyfingers and cream.
5. **Finish**: Dust generously with cocoa powder. Chill at least 4–6 hours (overnight best).

👉 Result: Balanced, creamy tiramisu with restrained sweetness and a boozy backbone.

🔑 Strategy

- Use **4 oz mascarpone** for tiramisu → balanced ratio with 10 ladyfingers.
