**C & H White Sugar** 

**Granulated INGREDIENTS: $1.99 expires in 4 days**

6 egg yolks 2 1/2 cups heavy cream

6 tablespoons white sugar, divided 2 tablespoons brown sugar

1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

**DIRECTIONS:**

1. Preheat oven to 300 degrees F (150 degrees C).
1. Beat egg yolks, 4 tablespoons white sugar and vanilla extract in a mixing bowl until \
   thick and creamy.
1. Pour cream into a saucepan and stir over low heat until it almost comes to boil. \
   Remove the cream from heat immediately. Stir cream into the egg yolk mixture; beat \
   until combined.
1. Pour cream mixture into the top pan of a double boiler. Stir over simmering water \
   until mixture lightly coats the back of a spoon, about 3 minutes. Remove mixture from \
   heat immediately and pour into a shallow heat-proof dish.
1. Bake in preheated oven for 30 minutes. Remove from oven and cool to room \
   temperature. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour or overnight.
1. Preheat oven to broil.
1. In a small bowl combine remaining 2 tablespoons white sugar and brown sugar. Sift this mixture evenly over custard. Place dish under broiler until sugar melts, about 2 minutes. Watch carefully so as not to burn.
1. Remove from heat and allow to cool. Refrigerate until custard is set again.
