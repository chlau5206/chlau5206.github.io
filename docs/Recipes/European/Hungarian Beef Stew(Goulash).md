**Hungarian Beef Stew "Goulash"**

---

Yield:

4 to 6 portions

**Ingredients**

* 8 slices bacon, chopped
* 2 large onions, thinly sliced,
  about 1 1/2 cups
* 1 tablespoon chopped garlic
* 2 pounds [beef shank](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/shank/index.html)
  or chuck, cut into stew meat
* Salt and pepper
* 3 tablespoons hot Hungarian paprika
* 1 teaspoon ground marjoram (Oregano)
* 1 teaspoon lemon zest
* 1/4 cup [white wine
  vinegar](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/vinegar/index.html)
* 1/2 cup white wine
* 1 cup [tomato
  puree](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/tomato-puree/index.html)
* 2 cups [beef broth](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/broth/index.html)
* 1 cup diced [potatoes](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/potato/index.html)

**Directions**

In the bottom of a heavy [casserole](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/casserole/index.html)
saute the chopped bacon to render the fat. Add the onion to the bacon fat and
brown until golden. Stir in the garlic but do not burn. Quickly add the beef in
one layer, season with salt and pepper and brown all sides well.

Sprinkle the paprika, marjoram and [lemon zest](http://www.foodterms.com/encyclopedia/zest/index.html) in
and quickly stir to coat the meat evenly.

Add vinegar and wine and cook until
nearly dry.

Add the tomato and broth, bring to a
boil quickly before lowering the heat to a simmer and cook gently for 45
minutes. Add the potatoes. Continue to cook for an additional 20 minutes before
serving with buttered noodles.
