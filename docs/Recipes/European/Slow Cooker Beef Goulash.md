# Slow Cooker Beef Goulash

## Ingredients

* 2 tbs olive oil
* 3 lb. beef stew meat, trimmed of
  fat
* 2 tsp. salt
* 1/2 tsp black pepper
* 3 tbs. flour
* 2 med. yellow onions, roughly
  chopped
* 4 garlic cloves, minced
* 3 tbs paprika
* 2 red bell peppers, diced
* 8 oz. can tomato sauce
* 2 cups beef broth
* 3 tbs fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Add olive oil to medium pan, over
   medium high heat.
2. Add beef stew meat to a large bowl
   and sprinkle with salt, pepper, and flour.
3. Stir to coat.
4. Brown beef stew meat in batches, in
   medium pan.
5. Add to slow cooker.
6. Add onions, garlic, and paprika to
   pan and cook over medium heat for 3-5 minutes and add to slow cooker.
7. Add diced bell peppers to slow
   cooker.
8. Add tomato sauce and beef broth to
   slow cooker, stir.
9. Cover and cook on low for 8-10
   hours, or on low for 4-5 hours.
10. Add fresh chopped parsley.
11. Serve over noodles or mashed
    potatoes.

Notes

1. Used a 6 quart slow cooker.
