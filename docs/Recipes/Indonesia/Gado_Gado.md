### 印尼加多加多醬(Gado Gado)（一至二人份量）

To prepare an authentic version, follow these key steps:
- **Sauce Preparation**: Fry peanuts, garlic, shallots, red chilies, and shrimp paste (_terasi_) until softened, then grind into a paste. Simmer this mixture with **sweet soy sauce** (_kecap manis_), tamarind, palm sugar, salt, and water until thickened.
    
- **Vegetable Prep**: Blanch green beans, cabbage, bean sprouts, and potatoes in salted water until tender-crisp. Cook hard-boiled eggs and fry tofu and tempeh separately until golden.
    
- **Assembly**: Arrange the blanched vegetables, fried tofu, tempeh, and eggs on a platter. Drizzle generously with the warm peanut sauce and garnish with **fried shallots** and **shrimp crackers** (_krupuk_).

**Gado-gado** is a traditional Indonesian salad and one of the country's five **national dishes** (designated in 2018 alongside _soto_, _sate_, _nasi goreng_, and _rendang_). The dish translates to "mix mix," reflecting its composition of **blanched or raw vegetables**, **fried tofu**, **tempeh**, **hard-boiled eggs**, and **lontong** (compressed rice cakes).
The defining feature is the **peanut sauce** (_bumbu kacang_), a rich dressing made from **ground roasted peanuts**, **palm sugar**, **chilies**, **garlic**, **tamarind**, and **shrimp paste** (_terasi_). Regional variations exist, such as **Gado-gado Betawi** from Jakarta (often using coconut milk) and **Gado-gado Surabaya** from East Java (typically spicier and served with crackers).

_**Gado-gado**_ ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language "Indonesian language") or [Betawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betawi_language "Betawi language")) is an [Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_cuisine#National_dishes "Indonesian cuisine") [salad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salad "Salad")[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gado-gado#cite_note-Balidaily-1) of raw, slightly [boiled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiled "Boiled"), [blanched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blanching_\(cooking\) "Blanching (cooking)") or [steamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steamed "Steamed") [vegetables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetable "Vegetable") and [hard-boiled eggs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard-boiled_eggs "Hard-boiled eggs"), boiled potato, fried [tofu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tofu "Tofu") and [tempeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempeh "Tempeh"), and sliced _[lontong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lontong "Lontong")_ (compressed cylinder rice cake wrapped in a banana leaf),[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gado-gado#cite_note-3) served with a [peanut sauce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut_sauce "Peanut sauce") dressing.
