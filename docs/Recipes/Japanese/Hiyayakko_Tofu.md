Hiyayakko
(cold tofu salad) Recipe

Cook Time: PT5 M

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

* 2 green onions
* ginger root, about 2"
* Tofu (400g)
* [Katsuobushi (dried bonito flakes)](http://www.japanesecooking101.com/dried-bonito-flakes/)
* soy sauce (or optionally [Ponzu
  Sauce](http://www.japanesecooking101.com/?p=2873))

Instructions

1. Slice green onions very thinly.
   Grate ginger.
2. Wash tofu under softly running
   water. Cut into 4 pieces.
3. Place a piece of tofu on a dish and
   top with some of the green onions, grated ginger, and Katsuobushi.
4. Pour some soy sauce or Ponzu Sauce
   on top.
