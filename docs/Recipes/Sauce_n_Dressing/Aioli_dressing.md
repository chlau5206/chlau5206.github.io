### Quick Method (Using Mayo)

**Ingredients**:   
	- 3/4 cup **mayonnaise**  
	- 5 finely grated **garlic** cloves  
	- 1-2 tbsp **lemon juice**  
	- 1/2 tsp **Dijon mustard**  

**Instructions**:

- Stir the **garlic**, **lemon juice**, and **Dijon mustard** together and let sit for 15 minutes to mellow the garlic flavor.
- Whisk in the **mayonnaise** until smooth.
- Season with **kosher salt** and additional **lemon juice** as needed.
