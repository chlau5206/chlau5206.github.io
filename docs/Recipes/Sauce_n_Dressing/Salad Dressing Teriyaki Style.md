Salad Dressing Teriyaki Style
=======================
Recipe #0   (my salad dressing)
1 tblsp  	Honey
1 tblsp 	Lemon/Lime Juice
1 tsp 		Wasabi
2 tsp 		Mayonnaise
1/2 tsp		Sesame Oil
* 1 tblsp 	Soy sauce (may not needed)

Recipe #1  (https://eatingrichly.com/teriyaki-salad-dressing/)
1 tblsp  	Honey
1 tblsp 	Rice Vinegar
2 tsp 		Mayonnaise
1 tsp 		Dijon Mustard
1/4 tsp		Sesame Oil

Recipe #2  (https://eatingrichly.com/teriyaki-salad-dressing/)
1 cup 		mayonnaise
10 tbsp 	rice vinegar
4 tbsp 		white granulated sugar
4 tbsp 		sesame oil
2 tbsp 		soy sauce
1/2 tsp 	garlic powder

Recipe #3  (https://www.powerplantpnw.com/blog/classic-teriyaki-restaurant-side-salad-dressing)
1/2 cup 	vegan mayo
1 tbsp 		toasted sesame oil
1 tbsp 		seasoned rice vinegar
1 tsp 		cane sugar
1 tsp 		soy sauce or tamari
1/4 tsp 	garlic powder
6-8 grinds	Fresh black pepper 
1 tbsp 		water
