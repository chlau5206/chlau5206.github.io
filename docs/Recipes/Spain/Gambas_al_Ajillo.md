**Gambas al Ajillo** is a classic **Spanish** tapas dish of shrimp cooked in **olive oil** with **garlic**, **paprika**, and **chili**. The name translates to "**shrimp with garlic**" (_gambas_ = shrimp, _ajillo_ = garlic). It is one of Spain’s most iconic and popular appetizers, especially in **Madrid** and **Andalusia**.

The dish is typically made by gently heating **extra virgin olive oil** with sliced or minced **garlic** and **dried red peppers** until fragrant. **Shrimp** are then added and cooked briefly until pink, often finished with a splash of **dry sherry** or **white wine** and fresh **parsley**. It is traditionally served sizzling hot in a small clay dish (_cazuela_) with **crusty bread** for dipping into the flavorful oil.
