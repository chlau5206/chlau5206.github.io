Instructions for Convection Oven(reduce 25F):
..................................................
1. Dough Preparation
	1.1 Prep & Temperature: Preheat your convection oven to 300F(150C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
	1.2. Dry Mix: Whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
	1.3. Wet Mix: Cream the softened butter and honey. Beat in the 2 eggs one at a time. Mix in the vanilla extract, orange zest, and 1 tablespoon of orange juice.
	1.4. Combine & Add-ins: Gradually add the dry mixture to the wet mixture, mixing until just combined. Fold in the almonds and chocolate. The dough should be soft but easier to handle than the 3-egg version.
2. First Bake (Log Formation)
	2.1. Shape: Divide the dough into two equal portions. Shape each portion into a log, about 10-11 inches long and 2 inches wide.
	2.2. Bake: Bake in the convection oven at 300F for 20-25 minutes (watch closely, as convection is faster), or until the logs are firm to the touch.
3. Second Bake (Slicing and Crispness)
	3.1. Cool & Slice: Remove the logs and let them cool for 15 minutes. Using a sharp serrated knife, slice diagonally into 1/2 inch thick slices.
	3.2. Crisp: Lay the slices cut-side down on the baking sheet.
	3.3. Finish: Return to the oven (still at 300F) and bake for 6-8 minutes per side, or until they are perfectly dry and crisp.

Notes:
..................................................
1. Anise Extract: Classic Italian biscotti are often made with anise extract. Feel free to add 1-2 teaspoons of anise extract or 2 teaspoons of anise seed (in addition to the vanilla extract) if you so desire.
2. Try four flavors of biscotti - almond, chocolate chip, pistachio lemon, and cranberry orange. Easy recipe for delicious homemade biscotti!

4. Butter: Since you are using butter, it is best to soften it (not melt) and cream it with the sugar. This incorporates air, which will make the resulting cookie slightly more tender than the oil version. The higher egg ratio in your base will compensate for the butter's moisture.
5. Temperature: 
	5.1. Preheat your oven to 325F (160C). This is a 25F reduction to prevent the honey from scorching. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
	5.2. Convection Oven TemperatureConvection ovens circulate hot air, cooking food faster and often resulting in better browning and crisping (which is perfect for biscotti!).1The general rule for converting a standard (conventional) baking temperature to convection is to reduce the temperature by 25F(or 15C).
		* Standard (Honey): 325F
		* Convection: 325F - 25F = 300F
